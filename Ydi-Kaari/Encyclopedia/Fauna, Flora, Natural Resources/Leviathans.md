The corrupted spawn of the Titan [[Umbravex]]. As varied as the creatures of the sea that they consume, the Leviathan are the colossal undersea beings that were born by the twisting [[Wyrd]] wielded by the Titan, unable to control their strange evolutions but bound to the consuming will of their progenitor, nonetheless.

The original Leviathan were birthed from the Form of the strange crustacean cephalopod asexually and were awakened to a powerful inheritance by [[Umbravex]]'s drive to consume. They abide in the deepest caverns and darkest trenches that lie along the bottom of the oceans, growing to massive size before their final years, in which they begin to transmute their enormousness into new polyps. After months of this, they eventually migrate to their own birthing grounds and give what remains of their bodies over to death as their egg sacs rupture and up to twelve new Leviathan swim forth. 

From their bloodlines come the sea serpents and marine ambushers that feed near the surface, as well as the the darker aberrations that have only ever been spotted at great distances by [[Aqualii]] scouts. 
They also share some form of the Titans blood with the greatest of monstrosities that roam the [[Evryn]] wilds; great offspring of [[Taerannon]] that were corrupted by Wyrd source.
