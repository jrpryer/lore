# Firearms
In the nation of progress, a new kind of weapon has been invented. It uses an explosive substance called black powder to propel a projectile over long distances, shredding through flesh and armor with terrifying ease. This is the firearm. 

#### What is a firearm? 
Firearms are a new category of weapons, separate from simple and martial weapons. Proficiency with simple and/or martial weapons does not grant a character proficiency with firearms. Firearms cannot be chosen for a character’s Weapon Mastery feature. Rather, to use a firearm’s mastery property, the character must take the Firearm Mastery feat presented below.

#### Tinker’s Tools 
Checks to make, adjust, or fix firearms require tinker’s tools.

### Firearm Feats 
The following feats will provide you with the ability to utilize Firearms. 

#### FIREARM TRAINING · Origin Feat 
Trained in the proper use and care of firearms, you gain the following benefits: 

***Firearm Proficiency.*** You gain proficiency with firearms. 

***Faster Reload.*** When you would make an attack as part of the attack action with a firearm that has the reload property, you may instead reload that firearm. You must have one free hand to reload a firearm. 

***Gunsmith.*** You gain proficiency with tinker’s tools. 

#### FIREARM MASTERY · Feat 
*Prerequisite:* 4th+ Level, proficiency with firearms, and the Weapon Mastery feature. 

A master in the use of firearms, you gain the following benefits: 

***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Dexterity or Intelligence score by 1, to a maximum of 20. 

***Quick Reload.*** You can use a bonus action on each of your turns to reload a firearm with the reload property. 

***Master of Firearms.*** You may add firearms to the list of weapons you may choose as part of your Weapon Mastery feature. Additionally, you may choose one additional weapon to apply your weapon mastery to, but it must be a firearm. 

#### Balance & Firearms 
Beware that Firearms do a lot of damage, and they do that damage at range. They are purposefully powerful mundane items your players can get, and they will drastically change how deadly your game feels at the table. Especially at lower levels. 

Consider avoiding using Firearms against low level characters, you’ll want to restrict their access to Advanced Firearms until they reach higher levels. 

When rewarding Firearms as loot, consider using the Firearm Malfunctions to nerf weapons they find on enemies to make sure they don’t change the balance of the whole game just as it’s starting.

#### Derringer 
A palm-sized, easily concealable, short-range flintlock pistol. 

#### Harper
A standard flintlock pistol, ideal as a side-arm, or to pair with a cutlass. 

#### Brown Bess 
This muzzle-loading smoothbore flintlock land pattern musket is the standard weapon of choice for the Atroxian military because of their reliability, range, and relatively low production cost.

#### Hunter 
This carbine variant of the Brown Bess is a lowercaliber, shorter barreled musket developed for hunters and scouts. 

#### Blunderbuss 
The blunderbuss is a firearm with a short, large caliber barrel which is flared at the muzzle. It’s a terrifying weapon at close range. 

#### Bayonet
A bayonet, once fixed onto a firearm with the two-handed property, the ensemble can be used as a simple melee weapon.

#### Leadball 
This small pouch of gunpowder attached to a smooth round lead ball is the proper ammunition for all simple firearms

#### Pepperbox 
A small pistol with four separate barrels, each loaded with its own round. 

#### Oldfield 
A single-action revolver with a turning cylinder containing multiple chambers. Before firing, the hammer must be cocked to align the barrel with the next round. 

#### Cleveland 
This bolt-action hunting rifle has an internal magazine, where you can load up to five cartridges which are handled in and out of the barrel chamber by manually operating a bolt via the bolt handle on the weapon’s right-hand side.

#### Oakenfall 
An experimental, heavy caliber breechloading firearm, used to hunt large game, such as oxen or elephants. An Oakenfall requires a special cartridge that costs and weighs twice as much as an ordinary cartridge. 

#### Dullwater 
A lever-action long-rifle which uses a lever built into the trigger guard to chamber the next round out of the large magazine hosted under the barrel. 

#### Cartridge 
A single cartridge where the charge and the bullet are packed into one jacket is the proper ammunition for all advanced firearms.

#### Doubleharper
A modified Harper with two barrels instead of just one. Uses leadballs as ammunition.

#### Doubledecker
A modified blunderbuss with two barrels instead of just one. Uses leadballs as ammunition.

#### Scattershot. 
When you make a ranged attack on your turn as part of the attack action, if you are wielding a Doubledecker with two rounds of ammunition remaining, you may instead choose to fire a scattershot. 

When you do, roll 2d20. If either d20 rolls equal to or below the misfire score, the gun suffers a misfire, and nothing happens. If it doesn’t misfire, each creature in a 15-foot cone must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Dexterity modifier) or take 5d4 piercing damage. This consumes both rounds of ammunition from the Doubledecker.

#### Flare Gun
A small pistol used for shooting flares into the sky as a distress signal. It uses flares as ammunition.
A flare fired by this weapon burns for 1 minute. While burning it sheds a bright light for 30 ft and dim light for a further 30 ft.
On a hit, the target takes 1d4 fire damage and starts burning (see the rules glossary.)

#### Handbomb
A small ball of pre-split metal wrapped around a charge of black powder. When the fuse is lit, the charge explodes and the ball splits into deadly flying shrapnel.

#### Dynamite
A single stick of dynamite. Each additional stick of dynamite bundled with the first adds 5ft to the radius up to a maximum of 20ft, and a die of damage up to a maximum of 10d6.

#### Powder Barrel
A large airtight barrel full of blackpowder. When exposed to fire, it explodes and deals 10d8 bludgeoning damage to each creature or object within 30 feet of it. A successful DC 12 Dexterity saving throw halves the damage.

#### Adrenaline
You can, as a bonus action, inject yourself or an adjacent creature, with a syringe containing adrenaline. An injected creature regains 2d4+2 hit points.

#### Compass
A flat disk with a metal needle that always points north.

#### Firecrackers
Using a utilize action, you can throw this explosive toy against a hard surface within 60 feet. Upon impact, the firecracker explodes and emits a boom that can be heard up to 100 feet away. Creatures within 5 feet of the explosion must make a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw or be blinded until the start of their next turn.

#### Fireworks
You can light a firework’s fuse with a utilize action. Once lit, the firework flies 100 ft in the direction it is pointed and explodes. 
Every creature within 10 ft of the explosion must make a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw or take 1d4 fire damage and become blinded until the start of their next turn.

#### Fuse
A length of fuse can be used to ignite a flammable or explosive substance from a distance. Applying a naked flame as a utilize action to any part of the fuse will cause it to ignite, and the flame will travel along the length of the fuse. 
A lit fuse will ignite highly flammable materials, such as paper, lantern oil or black powder, but not more stable materials such as wood. A lit fuse can be snuffed out with a DC 10 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check. A fuse burns at a rate of 10 ft per round.

#### Gas Mask
While wearing a gas mask with a filter, a creature automatically succeeds on saving throws made against harmful gases and vapors (such as stinking cloud effects or inhaled poisons).

#### Gas Mask Filter
A gas mask filter can filter harmful gases for up to 1 hour. You can take a utilize action to remove or install a gas mask filter.

#### Hand Flare
You can take a utilize action to light a hand flare, by striking it against a hard surface or by using a tinderbox. A hand flare burns for 1 hour, providing bright light in a 20-foot radius and dim light for an additional 20 feet. 

When you take the attack action, you can attack with the hand flare, using it as a simple melee weapon. On a hit, the target takes 1 fire damage.

#### Parachute
Constructed from cloth and sturdy rope and typically worn as a backpack. 
A creature can use its action to pull on a cord attached to the parachute, which causes it to spill open. If the creature is falling, the parachute fully opens and slows the creature’s fall to 60 ft per round, and it takes no damage from the fall, provided its size is medium or smaller. 

A parachute can only be used in this way if it is being worn or securely held. You can don or doff a parachute with a utilize action.

#### Tinker’s Tools
This set of tools includes a variety of hand tools, thread, needles, a whetstone, scraps of cloth and leather, and a small pot of glue. 
You can use tinker’s tools to craft firearms and ammunition or repair damaged firearms. 

Additionally, with 1 minute of work, you can render a firearm in your possession inoperable by removing key components and blocking vital mechanisms.

#### Watch
This clockwork device keeps accurate time, as long as it is wound once a day. It measures time in increments of seconds, minutes, and hours.

#### Artillery Firearms
The following firearms could be used as siege weapons or on naval vessels in your campaigns.

#### Gatler
A large machine operated firearm that uses an entire clip each round when fired.

***Gatler Volley.*** As an action, you can use a loaded Gatler to fire a volley. Each creature in a 60 ft. cone must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Dexterity modifier) or take 2d12 piercing damage, or half as much on a successful save. 

Prone creatures in the area that succeed their Dexterity save take no damage.

#### Long Nine
A small cannon, sometimes called a long nine, can be mounted at the bow of a ship to fire at other vessels as it chases them down. Uses nine-pound cannon balls as ammunition.

#### Broadsider
For the navy, a man-o-war’s primary method of assault is the broadside; a deadly volley of cannon fire meant to tear enemy vessels apart. 
The Broadsider cannon has served this purpose for decades. Uses twenty-eight-pound cannon balls as ammunition.

#### Cannon Ball
The ammunition for a cannon is a lead ball. Its cost depends on the weight, which of course depends on the intended weapon.

#### Grapeshot
An alternative ammunition for a Broadsider cannon. When a cannon loaded with grapeshot is fired, each creature in a 30 ft. cone originating from the cannon must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Dexterity modifier) or take 3d10 piercing damage, or half as much on a successful save. Prone creatures in the area that succeed their Dexterity save take no damage.

## Firearm Properties
Many weapons, including firearms, have special properties related to their use. Below are all the properties that may apply to firearms, including those already printed in the basic rules.

#### Ammunition
You can use a weapon that has the ammunition property to make a ranged attack only if you have ammunition to fire from the weapon. Each attack expends one piece of ammunition.

Unlike other ranged weapons, firearms must be loaded with the appropriate ammunition to be fired. You cannot recover fired bullets.

#### Artillery
Loading an artillery weapon requires one or more creatures to use a number of utilize actions equal to the weapon’s artillery score. You must have two free hands to load an artillery weapon. Once loaded, an artillery weapon can be aimed and shot like any other firearm.

#### Grenade
A type of weapon that is thrown to a point and explodes. Grenades have their range and area in parentheses, in that order, after the grenade property. 

When you take the attack action, you can replace one of your attacks with throwing a grenade.
Creatures in the area must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Dexterity modifier), taking the damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

#### Heavy
A heavy weapon is unwieldy compared to other weapons. You have disadvantage on your attack rolls with a heavy weapon if it’s a melee weapon and your Strength score isn’t at least 13 and if it’s a ranged weapon and your Dexterity score isn’t at least 13.

#### Light
When you take the attack action on your turn and attack with a light weapon, you can make one extra attack as a bonus action later on the same turn. That extra attack must be made with a different light weapon, and you don’t add your ability modifier to the extra attack’s damage, unless that modifier is negative.

For example, if you take the attack action on your turn and have an Oldfield in one hand and a Harper in the other—each of which has the light property—you can make one attack with each weapon, using the attack action and a bonus action, but you don’t add your Strength or Dexterity modifier to the damage roll of the bonus action, unless that modifier is negative.

#### Loading
You can fire only one piece of ammunition from a Loading weapon when you use an action, a Bonus Action, or a Reaction to fire it, regardless of the number of attacks you can normally make.

#### Misfire
Whenever you make an attack roll with a firearm, and the dice roll is equal to or lower than the weapon’s misfire score, the weapon misfires. The attack misses, and the weapon cannot be used again until you take a utilize action to try and repair it. 

To repair your firearm, you must make a successful Intelligence (Investigation) check with tinker’s tools (DC equal to 8 + the weapon’s misfire score). If your check fails, the weapon is broken and must be mended out of combat over a short or long rest at a quarter of the cost of the firearm. 

Creatures who use a firearm without being proficient increase the weapon’s misfire score by 1.

#### Range
A weapon that can be used to make a ranged attack has a range in parentheses after the ammunition or thrown property. The range lists two numbers. The first is the weapon’s normal range in feet, and the second indicates the weapon’s long range. 

When attacking a target beyond normal range, you have disadvantage on the attack roll. You can’t attack a target beyond the weapon’s long range.

#### Reload
The weapon can be fired a number of times equal to its reload score before you must take a utilize action to reload it. You must have one free hand to reload a firearm.

#### Siege Weapon
A siege weapon is a heavy piece of equipment built for sieges. Siege weapons do double damage to structures. They have an AC of 15 and 100 hit points. When reduced to 0 hit points, they break and no longer function. 

#### Two-Handed
A two-handed weapon requires two hands when you attack with it.

#### Thrown
If a weapon has the thrown property, you can throw the weapon to make a ranged attack, and you can draw that weapon as part of the attack. If the weapon is a melee weapon, you use the same ability modifier for that attack roll and damage roll that you would use for a melee attack with the weapon. 

For example, if you throw a handaxe, you use your Strength, but if you throw a dagger, you can use either your Strength or your Dexterity, since the dagger has the finesse property.


## Firearm Upgrades
Anyone tinkering with firearms might find themselves wanting to upgrade them to serve certain functions.

***Upgrade Cost.*** An upgrade costs N50,000 notes to apply and each upgrade applied to a firearm increases its misfire score by one.

***Upgrade Time.*** Upgrading a weapon can be done over the course of 40 hours. After that time, the tinkerer makes a DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) check with tinker’s tools. On a success, the upgrade is applied. On a failure, it takes another 40 hours before the upgrade is completed. A second roll is not required.

#### Stub
Ranged attacks made with this firearm while within 5 ft. of a hostile creature do not have disadvantage on the attack roll.

#### Muffled
This weapon’s barrel has several vents that release pressure in a gradual manner, significantly reducing the volume of the gunshot. Creatures more than 100 ft away can’t hear the sound of this weapon firing.

#### Scoped
Attacking at long range doesn’t impose disadvantage on your ranged attack rolls with this firearm. Installing this upgrade requires you to have a spyglass that you attach to the weapon as part of the upgrade process.

#### Lightweight
Removes the heavy property from a firearm. If the weapon does not have the heavy property, remove the two-handed property instead. If it has neither, give it the light property. You cannot apply this upgrade to a weapon more than once.

#### Kalterlock
You upgrade a weapon with the reload 1 property to instead be loading.


## Firearm Malfunctions
Looting firearms from the enemy may result in getting somewhat faulty firearms. Here are some faults that you might encounter.

#### Jammer
After firing this weapon, it jams and won’t fire again until you take a utilize action to unjam the weapon.

#### Finicky
Reloading this weapon always requires a utilize action.

#### Combustible
If this weapon misfires, it explodes. The wielder takes damage equal to the weapon’s damage dice and the firearm is destroyed.

#### Busted
When this weapon misfires, the misfire score increases by one.

#### Warped
Attacks made with this firearm have disadvantage.

#### Scrap
When this weapon misfires, roll 1d6 for a random malfunction from this list. You apply the malfunction to the firearm permanently. If the result is a malfunction already on the weapon, it’s destroyed.
