Smallfolk are diminutive humanoids who rely on community and cleverness to survive a world of big threats. These brave people bore both the loss of the Departure and atrocities of the Thinning rather well, as they were rarely categorized as a threat by larger peoples, or “bigfolk.”

All smallfolk share a near-supernatural affinity for the earth, and distinct groups of smallfolk are categorized by how they embraced that affinity. One group called **Gnomes** split off to build their communities in the urban sprawls of port towns and bustling cities. The other group, called **Halflings**, migrated to the open plains and vast countryside to bolter farming and herding communities. Regardless of where they choose to live, smallfolk possess boundless enthusiasm for improving the world around them. Their homes are filled with bounteous creature comforts resulting from both ingenuity and hard work.

While many smallfolk contentedly live their lives in cozy communities with others of their kind, a seemingly bottomless zest for life drives more adventurous members of this lineage to travel or settle in varied climates. Smallfolk fit in wherever they go, and troubles seem to roll off their backs magically—qualities that have ensured their survival throughout the years since the Departure.

# Traits
Your Smallfolk character has the following hereditary traits.
**Age**. Smallfolk reach adulthood around 20 and typically live to be around 150 years old, though Gnomes sometimes live twice as long.
**Size**. Your size is Small. Smallfolk are rarely more than 3 feet tall and average about 40 pounds.
**Speed**. Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
**Grounded**. Once per day, when you fail a save, you can reroll the die and use the new roll. Regardless of whether the new roll is a success or failure, you generate 1 [Luck.](https://play.shardtabletop.com/#book?id=mep8jxddbn9cfunu&fragment=fqxjo6q1mcshn5waa)
**Small Stature**. You can move through the space of any creature that is Medium or larger size. You can also attempt to hide when you are obscured by a creature of Medium or larger size.
**Natural Adaptation**. You inherited one of the following unique traits, determined by whether your characteristics are Halfling or Gnomish:
- ***Halfling***. When you fail a DEX save, you can spend 1 Luck to roll a d4 and add it to the save. You can only spend a Luck point this way once per roll. You can't use this trait while Prone or your speed is 0.
- ***Gnomish***. You know the _minor illusion_ cantrip. Choose whether INT, WIS, or CHA is your spellcasting ability for this spell during character creation. Additionally, you have advantage on all Intelligence saving throws against magic.
