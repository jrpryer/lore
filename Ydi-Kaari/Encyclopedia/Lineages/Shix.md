{Shisk of Pathfinder mixed Peri monks from the Corpus Angelus and some Owlin/Kenku inspirations}

Shix are learn, half-feathered humanoids who's history is passed down verbally and musically, rarely maintaining written records in order to keep their knowledge safe. Coupled with their fascination with collecting and protecting esoteric knowledge, they are often the truest conduits of the [[Passions & Apathies|Passions]], often taking up jobs that allow them to explore as many miles as possible.

Shix have features and skin-tones similar to [[Elvi#Diur'eli|Diur'eli Elvi]], usually ranging from deep tan to charcoal black. They are lighter than their body size might indicate, due to their hollow bones. Shix have no body hair—instead, their backs are covered in vestigial plumage that also extend to their elbows in the form of intricate pin feathers. Though Shix can't fly, they can tuck their necks and spread their mass out to glide at a reduced falling speed. Shix eyes also contract into slits in the sunlight and their irises are typically warmly colored, from hazel and brown to more unique colors like red or amber. 

Shix are quite varied in appearance, often carrying distinct feather patterns and appendage markings and have a unique ability to selectively pass on certain genetic traits to their offspring. Also, it is not uncommon for a single Shix to have multiple romantic partners in their short lifespan, allowing for large swings of visual distinction amongst even close family members. Size and musculature are the main traits that cannot be influenced and is the main way Shix identify their relations

Young Shix are often driven by curiosity to seek out far-flung sights and meet new people. Though such wanderlust may last for years, most return to their origin rookery later in life, where they often act as surrogate parents in their home territory for raising younger Shix. While all Shix contend with their base urges of curiosity and devotion to knowledge, they are quick to form bonds with those who show them compassion and loyalty, regardless of nationality or creed. Many are quite apt at mimicking the cultural identity of wherever they live to better treat with and learn from the locals.


Shix are born within rookeries, their culture molded from the surrounding society the rookery is in based in. Using building materials, mannerisms, languages, and ideals taken from those around them, their homes and culture look like a mottled  mimicry of the world around them, making no two rookeries—and thereby no two Shix—ever to be exactly alike. This reflects their curiosity and their love of keeping the secrets and stories of others.

# Traits
Your Shix character has the following hereditary traits.
**Age**. Shix reach adulthood at 10 years old, but live for only a short average lifespan of 50 years.
**Size**. Your size is either Medium or Small. Shix can range from 3.5 feet to just shy of 6 feet tall.
**Speed**. Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
**Secretkeeper**. Creatures have disadvantage on WIS (Insight) checks made to intuit what you are thinking or discern your motives.
**Glide.** When you fall at least 10 feet above the ground, you can use your reaction to extend your lighter feathered frame to glide horizontally a number of feet equal to your walking speed, and you take 0 damage from the fall. You determine the direction of the glide.
**Natural Adaption**. You gain one of the following traits determined by the inherited characteristics of your character:
- ***Mimicry***. As a *Ravenkin*, you can mimic any sound you’ve heard. When attempting to fool a creature into thinking the mimicked sound is real, make a CHA (Deception) check opposed by the creature’s WIS (Insight). On a success, you fool the listener into believing that the sound you created is real.
- ***Heightened Senses***. As an *Owlkin*, you have advantage on WIS (Perception and Investigation) rolls that rely on sight or hearing. You can see through lightly obscured areas normally and areas of dim light as if it were bright light. You are however more susceptible to overstimulation, giving you vulnerability to the Blinded and Deafened conditions.
