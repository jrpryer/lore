Ghoran are cultivated beings of wood, stone, moss, and flowers, breathed into sentience by the Titan [[Sylvania]] as the natural inhabitants of [[Evryn]] and [[Duhn]], before [[Nashtara]] brought the birth of the other species to their shores.
They perceive emotions like a synesthete experiences sound, connected through the Breath of their Titan creator to the soul of Kanyiirah itself. They were the first to whom Sylvania taught Druidic.

There are no direct settlements for Ghorans, as other lineages reckon them, but the Ghorans who awake from their heart trees inside the boundaries of single forest consider themselves to be of the same community. They further divide themselves into neighborhoods based on the species of their flora that make up their bodies, rather than geographic proximity.

A ternion formed of the oldest, the youngest adult, and the median Ghoran members of the neighborhood oversee all matters related to their species. There is little movement of the oldest member of each ternion, but the other two members traverse the isles often enough to keep their bodies from becoming stiff and stagnant. Each ternion of a forest meets regularly, once every 5 years, to discuss matters important to the larger Ghoran community.

Most Ghoran spend their time of walking either alone or with other members of their neighborhood. Ghoran adventurers are almost always young and considered to be thrill seekers and daredevils by their communities, though, they are urged to go out into the wider world to vent their exhilaration lest their emotions draw the unwanted attention of the [[Passions & Apathies|Apathies]] to their neighborhoods. These venturers are keen to move among the shorter-lived races and experience the pace of their lives and the rapid shifts of their emotions.

Once an old Ghoran moves into its final resting place, it becomes the trellis around a young tree that eventually envelops it, though many have claimed such trees retain some level of sentience and sing soft songs in Druidic that are carried on the wind. This is the reason Ghoran graveyards are referred to as 'choirs'.

In the early days of their existence, and around the time of [[Nashtara]], Ghoran were brightly mottled, boasting bright floral colors and sinewy bodies of lithe, plant-like structure. As time went on and their Gleamwood began to be settled by thier Alsied and Nagaji neighbors, their colors became more muted and their bodies more solid for labor and construction. Some were even trained in the art of defensive combat by [[Solanas]] himself.

Those that were chosen by the Kanirûl to travel and reside at Ech'Yinara felt, for the first time, accepted by this new generation of beings, seeing their presence and trust kept by these new gods as a tight bond between their old world and this new one. It is for this reason that they were so devout; not out of a sense of religious faith, but of a much deeper _need_ to be anchors of their world.
The eroding of that trust was reached however, after the last Ghoran of Ech'Yinara was slain in the Thinning wars, with no sight or hope of a Kanirûl return.

## Reserved Guardians
Ghoran were never overly numerous to begin with, mostly keeping to themselves in the Gleamwood even when the gods walked the isles. They were viewed as favored by the Kanirûl, since many of their lineage had been invited to live amongst the gods at the seat of their power in the condorium of Ech'Yinara.

Ghoran were already so few in number that those who died in self-defense during the Thinning were simply in the wrong place at the wrong time; Ech'Yinara was a bastion of the gods that was ransacked first by desperate zealots, then greedy thieves, then starved refugees of the wars. Ghoran are mysterious, but very emotional beings, holding out the longest in hope of the Kanirûl's return.

In the quiet, open glens of southern Duhn, merchant sailors may yet be treated with the sight of a Ghoran sentinel, standing watch on the edges of the Gleamwood, bearing its heavy wooden arms above its head in silent reverence to the Verdant Titan, holding strong to a hidden hope that the legacy of its people will not be forgotten.


# Traits
Your Ghoran character has the following hereditary traits.
**Age**. Ghoran age slowly in comparison to many humanoids. They reach adulthood around their 30th year and can extend their time of walking up to 400 years. 
**Size**. Your size is Medium. Like the trees they are born from, Ghorans come in a diverse range of heights and widths, standing between 5 and 7 feet tall, though rarely weighing more than 200 pounds.
**Speed**. Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
**Photosynthetic**. If you spend at least 1 hour in an area of sunlight or natural bright light, your daily food requirements are met (see [**Starvation and Dehydration**](https://play.shardtabletop.com/#book?id=ob05icggrgsjka2e&fragment=fsiz6mhlrb99rn5nk) in the _Player’s Guide_). In addition, if you spend the entirety a short rest in such an area, you can regain a number of expended hit dice up to your PB. Once you recover hit dice in this way, you can’t do so again until you finish a long rest.
**Hybrid Humanoid**. Though your type is Humanoid, you have traits in common with Plants. You have resistance to being Charmed, and advantage on saves against effects that would knock you Prone or make you fall Unconscious.
**Natural Magic**. Your innate connection to the Breath of Sylvania gives you an affinity with plants and animals. You know the _druidcraft_ cantrip. In addition, you can understand and verbally communicate with Beasts and Plants. This works like the _speak with animals_ spell, except it also applies to Plants.
**Living Wood**. In moments of peril, you can rapidly grow tough bark, vines, or similar growth to shield you from harm. When an attacker you can see would hit you with a melee attack, you can use your reaction to increase your AC by an amount equal to your PB against the triggering attack. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your PB, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.
