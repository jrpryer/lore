#spirits
Kitsune - (Beastkin, Agile / Tabaxi / Shifter / Leonin / Vulpin) hengeYokai limited shape shifting, spiritually attuned

Kitsune are shapeshifting beastkin whose hybrid forms bear the pronounced features of foxes, dogs, or cats—pointed ears, bushy tails, keen eyes, and graceful builds that blur the line between humanoid and beast. They alone possess the unique bloodline gift of hengeyokai transformation bestowed by [[Taerannon]], allowing them to shift their forms to either full animal or humanoid make ups for brief periods of time.

The Kitsune were driven to near-extinction during the Thinning wars, seen as favored by the Spirits of Kanyiirah and exploited for their potent spellcasting. They were recruited and used by their Urik commanders and leaders as conduits of magic on the battlefield, which also made them primary targets.

Kitsune were trapped just as much by their enemies as their own commanders; their shapeshifting abilities lacked practiced longevity and could not be maintained for long durations, making escape from the battlefield possible, but not probable. Any Kitsune that managed to escape the bloodshed were quickly discovered again and pressed into the service of another warlord or rising faction, being deemed too valuable to the war efforts to be left alone. And yes, that frustration led many to adopt a code of extreme "scorched-earth" conduct that led to their destruction.

Their brief shapeshifting abilities created a cruel irony, as they were powerful enough to be valuable, but not powerful enough to ensure their own extrication from danger. Their defensive, violent pushback to this treatment reflects the psychology of cornered survivors choosing destruction over continued exploitation.
Current Kitsune survivors in hiding harbor extreme mistrust of authority figures and often refuse to help anyone if they are explicitly sought out for their abilities.

# Traits
Your Kitsune character has the following hereditary traits.
**Age.** Kitsune mature reach adulthood by age 18. Most live to about 90 years, though their connection to the spirit world keeps them sharp-minded until near the end.
**Size.** Your size is Medium. In your natural hybrid form, Kitsune stand between 4 and 6 feet tall with lithe builds, pronounced vulpine features, large pointed ears, and bushy tails. You rarely exceed 140 pounds.
**Speed.** Your base walking speed is 35 feet. Your claws and reversed joints also grant a climbing speed equal to your walking speed. 
**Darkvision.** You have darkvision to a range of 60 feet.
**Spirit-Touched.** You can sense the  presence of unbound spirits within 30 feet of you, though not their exact location or nature, as well as feel the pressure of Greater Spirits within that same range.
	**Hengeyokai Transformation.** You can use an action to bodily shift into one of two alternate forms for up to 1 hour. You can end either transformation early as an action. Once the transformation ends or if you drop to 0 hit points, you shift to your natural hybrid form. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, regaining all expended uses when you finish a long rest:
- ***Animal Form*.** You transform into a vulpine, canine, or feline. Your size becomes Small, your walking speed becomes 40 feet. You retain your ability scores, but you cannot speak, cast spells, wield weapons, or manipulate objects that require hands. Any equipment that is carried or worn when you shift merges into your new form.
- ***Humanoid form*.** You transform into a Medium or Small humanoid appearance (such as an Elvi, Oread, Smallfolk, etc.). You can adjust height, weight, and general features, but your statistics remain the same and you retain a vestige marker of your lineage (tail, nose, ears, etc.) leaving them faintly visible (DC 15 Perception or Investigation check to be noticed).
**Natural Weapons.** You have sharp claws and fangs that serve as natural weapons, which you can use to make unarmed strikes. If you hit, you deal **1d6** + your STR or DEX modifier damage (choose which during character creation) of type slashing (claws) or piercing (fangs).
