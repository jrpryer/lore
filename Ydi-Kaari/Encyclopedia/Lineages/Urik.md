Forged in the fires of conflict. Uriks often revel in testing their strength against worthy foes.
Before [[The Departure]] many noble Urik houses were favored by [[Tympestyr]], becoming rapt students of his early alchemical pursuits and destructive Arcane enchantments. Though they are few, they still retain closely guarded family secrets of these employments entrusted to them by the crafty god of storms. 

Uriks and [[Syderean]] were the first [[Nashtara|Nashtaran]] people groups to take on core leadership roles under direct instructions from the Kanirûl in the early years. They structured themselves into noble houses, 3 of which were quite powerful in Cara at the time of [[The Departure]]. These took it upon themselves to teach and train specific swathes of Carain Hobgoblins into the most elite military force the isles had ever seen. %%They were NOT as a people group the exclusive aggressors during the Thinning wars, they simply leveraged their knowledge and favor from the gods more appropriately. No people group in Cara-Duhn (save the Aqualii) did things on their own in a vacuum; the early cities were established by the walking gods, and then later new cities were founded by great leaders and movements for resource accumulations. %% 
This seeming stroke of genius unfortunately ended up painting a bigger target on them though, once the Thinning wars began dragging on and cities states were being ransacked and burned: most of the major capitals were controlled by Urik noble houses at the time of the Departure.

Most Urik are wanders by choice, as they seek ways of rediscovering their place in the world without their positions of power, while also protecting the secret knowledge and enchantment techniques of their houses. Only some are interested in accepting the recompenses given by the other races, choosing to seek a revitalization of their noble glory through leadership.

The Urik were some of the first nobility of Cara-Duhn, tasked by the Kanirûl to lead and organize by example. Their culture reflects this legacy, exhibiting that true power lies in decisive action, be it measured or brutal: demand respect, hone your skills to mastery, complete the job. 

Urik nobility taught that mastery comes through refinement. A perfect alchemical formula. A flawless stratagem. A skin hardened by callouses and scars. They believe violence without control is wasted strength—and strength without purpose is shameful. In their reduced circumstances, they cling this discipline; to abandon it would be to abandon hope for a better world. They leave little space for leisure in their lives and thus their culture has no reserve of faith to call upon when in dire straits; better to be defeated in pursuit of highest ideals than rest on one's haunches and wait for a lazy death.

**Honor Duels**: In noble Urik culture, insults demand response. A duel of wits precedes a duel of blades. They perfected the art of issuing shrewd challenges and coolly wielding lethal intent, a tradition born from centuries of inter-house rivalry that wouldn't risk open war. Modern Uriks extend this similar courtesy to others, which often disarms those expecting a more brutish confrontation. When that courtesy is violated, however, the response is swift, devastating, and absolute.

# Traits
Your Urik character has the following hereditary traits.
**Age**. Uriks reach adulthood by 16 years old and seldom live longer than 100 years. However, Uriks remain in their prime for most of their lives, undergoing few physical changes or signs of aging between middle age and the end of their natural lifespan.
**Size**. Your size is Medium. Uriks stand between 6 and 8 feet tall and commonly range from 110 to 350 pounds in weight.
**Speed**. Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
**Stalwart**. When you are subjected to an effect that requires you to make a save at the end of your turn, you can instead choose to make the save at the start of your turn.
**Orcish Endurance**. When you are reduced to 0 Hit Points but not killed outright, you can drop to 1 Hit Point instead. Once you use this trait, you can’t do so again until you finish a Long Rest.
Additionally, when you would die due to suffocating or gaining levels of exhaustion, you instead enter a death-like stasis. While in stasis you are incapacitated, can’t move, can’t speak, and are unaware of your surroundings. You also cease to age, and your body is protected from decay. You can remain in this state until you are restored by mundane or magical healing, or your body is completely destroyed.
