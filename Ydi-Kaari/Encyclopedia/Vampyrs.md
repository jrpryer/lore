The Elvi of Rathus' family who partook in [[Fate of the Heartkindler|Valencia's murder]] and underwent the [[Laws of True Magic|Second Law Convergence]] that transformed them into their final monstrous, [[SECRETS#True Laws of True Magic|Instinctual]] forms, marking their bloodline as baned by the blackest of curses: 
![[Fate of the Heartkindler#^f5a647]]

The power of Myst's fragment granted the Rathus bloodline naturally attractive features while enhancing their bodies in supernatural ways. Their connection to others is stronger, bonds formed through marriage, lust, or oath are neigh unbreakable, magically empowered by the very blood in their veins. Consequently, they can influence others with their charm before enslaving them to their will. Though all falls away into meaningless chaff in the shadow of their curse: they are slaves to their hunger for blood. The burning need never truly vanishes in their bellies, always urging them to feed and relish in the death of other beings. They are some of the truest monsters ever to walk the shores of the great isles.

# House Sangyr
The four respectable vampyr houses collectively adopted the name **Sangyr** upon their emergence into Theocracy society, approximately 150 years after the Thinning's end. The name—meaning "of the blood"—was carefully chosen to suggest noble bloodline heritage while subtly acknowledging their curse. It provides administrative legitimacy and distances them from the Fifth House, which retained the name **Rathus** in defiance of respectability.

## RATHUS
**Epithet:** the Thief
**House:** Fifth House Rathus (Patriarch)  
**Age:** 166
**Status:** In hiding, location unknown

**Appearance:** Gaunt and predatory, with sunken eyes that gleam with unsettling intelligence. His features remain handsome in that supernaturally refined way all vampyrs possess, but twisted by a century of dark magical experimentation. Dresses in practical, travel-worn clothing—a philosopher-monster, not a courtier.

**Philosophy:** Rathus remains convinced his crime was not sacrilege but ambition meeting opportunity. The Kanirûl were mortals elevated through First Law Convergence with Primordial power—if they could ascend, why not him? The curse is merely a technical flaw in an otherwise perfect theft. Valencia's divine essence flows through his bloodline; he simply needs to master it.

**Current Activities:** Conducts blood magic experiments in the furthest reaches of Cara-Duhn's wilderness, pursuing "completion of ascension." His research has produced Lycanthropy (weaponized from a Shurgen-Tur corpse), the Hags of the Silver Hills, and the fanatical Blood Witches. He views each creation as a stepping stone toward true divinity through mastery of Second Law Convergence between Divine and Wyrd sources.

**Motivations:** Transcendence through transgression. He seeks to prove that mortality can seize immortality through will and knowledge alone. The fact that four of his sons "hide among cattle" disgusts him—they had godhood in their grasp and settled for being cursed nobles.

**Personality:** Coldly philosophical, utterly without remorse, capable of waiting centuries for technology to mature. Speaks of Valencia rarely but always smugly, as one discusses a research project that holds great potential. Patient, brilliant, and monstrously detached from conventional morality.

**Relationships:**
- Has not spoken to [[#Dylanus]] in 70 years
- Corresponds with [[#Corvus]] regularly about magical theory
- Views [[#Sateus]] as his only true heir
- Considers his other sons acceptable losses if they're discovered

**Quote:** _"The Kanirûl stole their divinity from the Primordial depths. I merely stole from thieves. The curse proves the power is real—it simply requires... refinement."_

## SATEUS
**Epithet:** the Black
**House:** Fifth House (First born)  
**Age:** 139
**Status:** Rathus's enforcer and right hand

**Appearance:** Tall, powerfully built, moves with predatory grace. Carries a black blade that drinks blood and never dulls—custom-forged by a long-dead master smith he subsequently killed and consumed. Dresses in dark, practical combat attire. His eyes are the coldest of all the brothers.

**Philosophy:** The curse made them better. A century and a half of training has made him one of – if not *the* – greatest swordsman in Cara-Duhn; no mortal could challenge him now. This is natural selection at divine scale. They are apex predators; pretending otherwise is weakness masquerading as pragmatism.

**Combat Prowess:** Unparalleled duelist. His swordsmanship is religious devotion to excellence. He's killed more people than any other vampyr—always in "fair" duels where he reveals his nature first. It's not about feeding; it's about dominance. About proving superiority.

**Motivations:** Perfection through endless refinement. He trains obsessively because he fears obsolescence—the day his years of practice might be overcome.

**Personality:** Disciplined to asceticism. Speaks rarely but with absolute conviction. Views combat as the only honest interaction between beings. Despises his brothers' respectability as cowardice. Loyal to Rathus because they share conviction: they are what they are, and that's magnificent.

**Relationships:**
- Deep, bitter rivalry with [[#Talis]] (they fought to a draw once; both want a rematch; Dylanus forbids it)
- Contemptuous of [[#Caelum]]'s guilt
- Respects [[#Corvus]]' dedication to research
- Views [[#Dylanus]], [[#Valens]], and [[#Rhilos]] as acceptable targets if they threaten the Fifth House

**Secret Fear:** That one day, someone will be better. That his four hundred years of perfection will meet something that surpasses him. This fear drives him to ever-greater extremes of training.

**Quote:** _"My brothers hide among sheep, pretending to be shepherds. I am what we became—the wolf that no longer apologizes for hunger."_

## DYLANUS
**Epithet:** the White
**House:** First Sangyri House (Second born, Head)
**Age:** 136
**Status:** Public face of vampyr legitimacy, de facto leader of the four houses

**Appearance:** Strikingly handsome in the supernatural way all vampyrs possess, but cultivated to approachability. Wears white and silver—deliberately chosen irony. Every gesture choreographed, every word tested. Presents as the cursed noble seeking redemption through service.

**Political Genius:** Recognized earliest that brute force couldn't sustain them. The Thinning taught him that monstrous behavior invites monstrous response. So he became what the Church wanted to see: victims of their curse, seeking salvation through devotion and wealth.

**The Long Game:** Orchestrated the four houses' emergence, coached his brothers on presentation, positioned them as essential to Theocracy stability _before_ revealing their nature. His genius is understanding that the Church doesn't want to destroy them—it wants to _save_ them. So he gives them a project: wealthy families desperately seeking a cure, willing to fund anything.

**Motivations:** Legitimacy through institution-building. He wants to cure the curse not for redemption but because mortality limits political power. If they could walk in sunlight, breed true heirs, and die naturally at 800 like Elvi, they'd rule Cara-Duhn within three generations.

**Personality:** Charismatic without warmth, calculated without coldness. Brilliant at reading rooms and people. Views mortals as pieces on a game board—not with contempt, but with a strategist's detached interest. Every conversation is a negotiation. Every smile costs something.

**Relationships:**

- Chairs the monthly four-house coordination meetings
- Tense relationship with Caelum (needs his piety, resents his guilt)
- Collaborative but distant with Valens (they need each other, neither likes the other)
- Relies on Talis for military legitimacy
- Secretly fears Corvus's research will expose them all
- Views Rathus as existential threat to everything he's built

**Secret Truth:** Has a contingency plan to kill Rathus if necessary. Has never told his brothers. Rhilos knows anyway.

**Quote:** _"The Church built itself on the love between Valencia and Aesuhir. We built ourselves on her corpse. The architecture is stable—provided no one examines the foundation."_

## TALIS
**Epithet:** the Wall
**House:** First Sangyric House (Third born, sworn to Dylanus)
**Age:** 134
**Status:** Military commander, leader of the Scarlet Company

**Appearance:** The largest of the brothers, built like his epithet suggests. Battle-scarred despite vampyric healing—he's been injured badly enough that even immortal flesh remembers. Wears practical military gear, Scarlet Company insignia prominent. Moves with economy of motion that suggests immense strength held in check.

**Military Mastermind:** Where Sateus is a duelist, Talis is a general. Commands the **Scarlet Company**—an elite mercenary force of Dhampir from all four houses. They're the families' unified military arm, operating outside direct Church command structure as "private contractors."

**The Scarlet Company's Purpose:**

1. Military legitimacy through effectiveness
2. Dhampir control through institutional loyalty
3. Deniability for Theocracy's dirty work
4. Integration that ensures survival

**Strategic Philosophy:** Ran the mathematics early: four vampyr houses vs. the entire Theocracy? They lose. Four houses _as_ the Theocracy's enforcers, creditors, and power brokers? They win without fighting. Force projection plus strategic positioning equals survival.

**Zstalfo Granetoss Connection:** Cultivated the prominent Chassurate carefully over decades. Provides Scarlet Company support for Inquisitorium operations, advises on military strategy. Has quietly made himself indispensable. Granetoss believes he's using the vampyr's expertise; Talis is using Granetoss to make the House military power irreplaceable.

**Motivations:** Stability through overwhelming deterrence. Make the houses so militarily valuable that destroying them becomes strategically impossible.

**Personality:** Blunt, pragmatic, prefers actions to words. Has a code—protects those under his command, honors promises made in combat. This reputation is carefully cultivated and absolutely genuine. Loyalty must be earned even from cattle. Doesn't romanticize what they did; guilt is waste, regret is poison, survival is victory.

**Relationships:**

- Deep rivalry with Sateus (fought to a draw once, both want rematch)
- Respects Dylanus's strategy, executes his plans
- Protective of his Dhampir soldiers—they're his, and he keeps his tools sharp
- Distrusts Corvus's research but recognizes its value
- Contemptuous of Caelum's endless guilt

**The Scarlet Compact:** All Dhampir who join swear an oath empowered by vampyr blood magic. It creates bonds that supersede individual sire control within military context. Has accidentally created a Dhampir culture that identifies more with the Company than individual houses. Talis monitors this carefully—useful now, potentially threatening later.

**Quote:** _"Reputation is currency. The Scarlet Company has one worth more than gold: brutal but fair, merciless but honorable. In a world that knows what we did, reputation is survival."_

## CAELUM
**Epithet:** the Nameless Penitent
**House:** Second Sangyric House (Fourth born, Head)  
**Age:** 131
**Status:** Bishop of the Benedictiary, penitent scholar

**Appearance:** Gaunt from frequent fasting, prematurely aged-looking despite vampyric immortality (constant self-denial shows even on undead flesh). Wears simple clerical robes, no ornamentation. His eyes carry permanent haunting—he met Valencia's gaze in her final moments.

**The Broken Brother:** Of all the brothers, only Caelum truly broke that night. Something in Valencia's dying gaze shattered him differently. He renounced his birth name, spent two decades in isolation feeding only when starvation forced him, and joined Dylanus's plan not from pragmatism but desperate need for purpose beyond horror.

**Theological Prison:** As Bishop of the Benedictiary, he's close enough to witness divine arts daily, permanently barred from higher positions that require such blessings. Valencia could have granted his line divine favor. He helped tear out her heart instead. Every blessing ceremony is a fresh wound.

**Scholarly Penance:** Authors theological papers under his own name (unlike Corvus's pseudonyms—Caelum's self-flagellation is public). His work on Vólat-rah doctrine is brilliant because he studies divine grace from permanent exile from it. Three major texts, all bearing the name "the Nameless" as author.

**The Knowledge That Breaks Him:** He knows exactly what they did. He remembers her face, her pleas, the taste of divine flesh. And he knows the Theocracy was founded partly to honor Aesuhir's love for her. Every sermon praising their union is acid in his mouth. Every "Departed Heartkindler" reference is a lie by omission to people who trust him.

**Motivations:** Atonement through service, knowing it's ultimately futile. There is no redemption, only the work of penance until Final Silence claims him—if it ever does.

**Personality:** Quietly intense, intellectually ferocious, emotionally devastated. Speaks carefully, weighs every word. Fasts frequently, pushing hunger to its limit. When he feeds, he weeps every time; even after a century of this, he can't punish himself enough.

**Relationships:**

- Resents being used by [[#Dylanus]] but lacks strength to refuse
- Unexpected friendship with [[#Rhilos]]: they correspond about meaning and damnation
- The other brothers find his guilt disturbing—useless and performative
- Avoids [[#Rathus]] entirely
- Cannot bear to look upon Valencia's sigil

**The Terrible Truth:** He's the only one who suspects there's more to discover about their curse's specificity—not just _that_ they killed a goddess, but _what_ that goddess represented magically. He's afraid to investigate. If he confirms it, his entire theological framework collapses.

**Quote:** _"I serve the Church built on the love I murdered. Every mass I conduct, every blessing I witness, every prayer I lead—all of it scaffolding around a corpse. Her corpse. And still I cannot stop."_

## CORVUS
**Epithet:** the Ash
**House:** Third Sangyric House (Fifth born, Head)  
**Age:** 127
**Status:** Master arcanist, theoretical researcher

**Appearance:** Silver-gray hair (unusual for vampyrs, possibly from magical exposure), piercing analytical gaze. Hands perpetually stained with alchemical residue. Wears practical robes with countless pockets for components. Moves with distracted precision—always mentally elsewhere.

**Former Student of Tympestyr:** Studied under the Stormbreaker briefly before the curse. Showed talent for evocation and transmutation, earned three months of attention, then was dismissed for asking too many questions about the boundaries between life and death. Tympestyr called him "morbidly ambitious." Corvus considered it a compliment.

**Magical Fascination:** The murder and transformation didn't horrify him—it _fascinated_ him. A Second Law Convergence between Divine and Wyrd, anchored through theft of Valencia's Myst fragment, refracting through eight corrupt souls simultaneously. From magical theory perspective: breathtaking. That it damned them was almost incidental.

**Research Focus:** Conducts careful experiments on their curse—not to break it, but to _understand_ it. Every Dhampir born to his line is a data point. Every feeding carefully logged. Has mapped vampyr blood reactions to seventeen forms of divine magic, twelve silver alloys, twenty-three plant species mentioned in Valencia's rituals.

**Secret Project:** Terrifyingly close to replicating vampirism through ritual magic—no goddess-murder required. If he succeeds, he'll prove the curse can be systematized, codified, reproduced. What he'll do with that knowledge even he doesn't know. The discovery itself is the goal.

**Motivations:** Understanding through dissection. The curse is the most interesting research question in Cara-Duhn. Everything else is secondary.

**Personality:** Clinically detached, speaks in hypotheticals, treats conversation like academic debate. The most _alien_ of the brothers—drifted furthest from humanoid emotional frameworks. Not cruel, just absent of normal empathy. Views his own damnation as the price of knowledge.

**Relationships:**

- Only brother who still visits the Fifth House regularly
- Corresponds with Rathus about magical theory
- Provides Rathus with research findings; Rathus provides test subjects
- Neither acknowledges this arrangement to other brothers
- Rhilos knows, of course

**Dhampir Breeding Program:** Most disturbing of the four houses. Breeds Dhampir in controlled conditions, varying mothers' bloodlines, testing transformation methods. Many don't survive childhood. Survivors are walking experiments—decades-long studies in vampyr genetics and the nature of the curse.

**Quote:** _"Ethics are frameworks for creatures with limited lifespans. We have centuries to pursue knowledge. The question isn't 'should we have done it'—it's 'what does the doing teach us about the fundamental nature of magic and divinity?' I submit that the data has been invaluable."_

## VALENS
**Epithet:** the Gilded
**House:** Fourth Sangyric House (Sixth born, Head)  
**Age:** 124
**Status:** Economic mastermind, cultural patron

**Appearance:** Exquisitely dressed in the height of fashion, jewelry that costs more than small estates, perfect grooming. Embodies wealth made flesh. Every gesture refined, every word chosen for aesthetic impact. Wears gold extensively—literal gilding.

**Economic Empire:** Four decades of compound interest, strategic investment, and ruthless business practice. Vampyric capital is woven into every major Theocracy enterprise—universities, churches, trading companies, noble estates. Follow the money far enough, you find Valens.

**Cultural Patron:** Genuinely cultured, not from pretense but because beauty and knowledge are the only things four centuries hasn't dulled. Hosts the grandest salons in Virtue. Artists, philosophers, nobles mingle. Some know what he is. Most suspect. None care—his patronage is worth the risk.

**Feeding Innovation:** His "servants" are handsomely compensated long-term employees with retirement plans. They line up to work for him. Some volunteer their blood. Why hunt when you can purchase? Why terrorize when you can negotiate? Cattle can be bred for quality, fed well, kept productive.

**Motivations:** Wealth as immortality's true potential. Time without resources is imprisonment. What matters is what you can _do_ with eternity. He's built something that will outlast him—an economic empire that makes the vampyrs indispensable.

**Personality:** Sophisticated, aesthetically obsessed, exquisitely mannered. Treats every interaction as performance art. Views mortals as resources to be managed well—enlightened self-interest, not kindness.

**Secret Vice:** Addicted to risk. A century and a half of accumulation bore him. Makes deliberately dangerous investments—backing rebellious artists, funding heretical research, supporting political radicals—just to feel the thrill of potential loss. Playing an increasingly dangerous game. Some part of him wants to lose.

**Relationships:**

- Collaborative but distant with Dylanus (they need each other, neither likes the other)
- Finds Caelum's guilt tedious
- Respects Talis's pragmatism
- Avoids Corvus (finds his experiments distasteful—bad for branding)
- Amused by Rhilos's information networks

**Dhampir Program:** Largest population, best treated. His labor force—accountants, managers, estate staff. Many mortals volunteer for breeding programs because Dhampir children inherit positions. Has accidentally created a Dhampir middle class who see servitude as privilege. Most insidious version—they thank him for bondage.

**Quote:** _"We paid the highest price imaginable for immortality and power. A century later, the return on investment has been extraordinary. The guilt? That's merely overhead—and I've always been excellent at managing costs."_

## RHILOS
**Epithet:** the Spider
**House:** Fifth House Rathus (Seventh born, youngest)  
**Age:** 120
**Status:** Intelligence operative, information broker

**Appearance:** Small—barely 1.6 meters—slender even for a vampyr. Easily overlooked, deliberately so. Dresses to blend into backgrounds. Moves quietly, observes constantly. His eyes miss nothing.

**Transformation Through Necessity:** The overlooked youngest, physically dominated by his brothers. The Thinning taught him: the deadliest weapon isn't strength, but knowledge. He rebuilt himself as the Spider—webs of informants, networks of blackmail, surveillance systems spreading across Cara-Duhn.

**Information Empire:** Knows which Centenary councilmembers have mistresses. Which Pulette Knights embezzle. Which scholars plagiarized theses. Where every vampyr safe house is located. Which Dhampir children might betray their sires. His intelligence network serves Rathus first, but Rhilos sells information to his other brothers—carefully, never compromising the Fifth House, always at calibrated prices.

**Greatest Fear:** That information is his only value. That without his networks, his brothers would discard him. He's right—Rathus keeps him alive because he's useful. The day he stops being useful is the day Sateus gets a target.

**Motivations:** Proving value through indispensability. He survives by being too dangerous to kill and too useful to discard.

**Personality:** Soft-spoken, easily underestimated, observes everything. Asks questions that seem innocent until you realize what he's learned. Never forgets anything—vampyric memory weaponized. Polite to servility when face-to-face, ruthless in practice.

**Feeding Habits:** Exclusively feeds on people who've betrayed him or his network. It's personal for Rhilos. The hunger is convenient excuse.

**Secret Hobby:** Writes poetry. Beautiful, aching anonymous verses about loneliness and observation. Published under two dozen pseudonyms. Three have won minor literary prizes. Never told anyone. It's the only thing that's _his_.

**Relationships:**
- Unexpected friendship with Caelum: they correspond about meaning and damnation—carefully coded theological letters that are actually philosophical dialogues. He is the only member of the Fifth House Caelum trusts wouldn't kill him, though neither acknowledges this.

**The Insurance Policy:** Rhilos maintains detailed records of everything—every crime, every secret, every vulnerability of every family member. Multiple copies hidden across Cara-Duhn. If he dies, they're released. His brothers suspect these exist, but none dare verify for risk of losing their precarious edge.

**Quote:** _"We share the greatest secret in Cara-Duhn. This binds us more permanently than any blood we may share, therefore our insurance isn't optional. I have great pains and gone to great lengths to ensure it stays that way."_


## The Fifth House of Rathus
Unlike the four respectable houses, Rathus, Sateus, and Rhilos retained the original family name, rejecting the "Sangyr" rebranding as capitulation. They operate from hidden locations in the Carain wilderness, conducting blood magic experiments that have produced:
- **Lycanthropy:** Weaponized disease crafted from a Shurgen-Tur corpse
- **The Hags of the Silver Hills:** Twisted orphans, traumatized and magically warped
- **Blood Witches:** Perfected version of the Hag experiments, fanatically loyal

The Fifth House is despised by the four respectable houses, who see Rathus's activities as existential threat to their careful political positioning.
