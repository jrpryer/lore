> The year is 97 [[TIMELINE.canvas|SD]]

Upon the sphere of [[Kanyiirah]] there lies the continental paradise known as [[Cara-Duhn]]. A collection of major and minor islands sporting many different biomes and landmarks, these Sacred Isles are a region of both unity and chaos; a land on the brink of a great event that will spell either greatness or disaster for those that call it home. Neigh brews a violent and stormy season, when the poignant actions of even a meager few can alter the course of nations.

### This is a swashbuckling, treasure-hunting, island-hopping, Pirating adventure on the high seas.

The richness of the lesser isles is alluring to any that might find themselves ostracized or cast out from "civilized" society. Your life consists of ambushing rich trade routes, running from heavy gunned navies, risking life and limb for lost treasure, and exploring the dangers of the oceanic wilderness. 

From a modern-day perspective, the technological era of this campaign takes place around the late 1600's in the Age of Sail, with Renaissance-esk technologies like alchemy and black powder introducing a new and dangerous tipping of the economic and militaristic scales of power. Read the [world meta](https://www.worldanvil.com/w/kanyiirah-jrpryer/meta) for a more in-depth look.

## Character Creation
When building your character, make these important selections *(in no particular order)* as early as you can:
- **Lineage**: Choose one of the available races from the [[Cara-Duhn|Cara-Duhnian People]] page
	- Note that, if you find another ancestry/lineage with traits/abilities that you really like, still make a selection from the campaign's Lineage list, as that will be your true appearance to the rest of the world. Your GM can work with you on what the statistics can look like behind the scenes after that

- **Heritage**: Select what feels most thematically appropriate. This will represent the culture that you were raised in and around, setting the general surroundings that molded you. With a thorough enough timeline, just about anything is possible here. Again, if you find something more appealing than the options provided, work with your GM to take on a generic Is'Deli Brotherhood heritage and work in your desired elements

- **Background**: Backgrounds represent what your character did as an individual before they took up the adventuring life, giving your character skill proficiencies, and talents. Ideally, anything that would solidify you place as a pirate, scallywag, refugee, or dubious individual is encouraged. Check with your GM to see if any archetypal talents can be granted early, to lock in the pirate theme. There is a generic Corsair option that fits nicely if you can't / don't wish to pick

- **Class**: All Tales of the Valiant classes are available. Party balance will *not* be enforced, as the preferred player style of the campaign — **Seekers**, **Solders**, or **Schemers** — will be determined through gameplay of the early sessions. If your core character concept is already realized with the Lineage, Heritage, and Background choices, then you might collect your **Ability Scores** first and let them inform this choice

> A Note on **Weapons**: Most firearms are fair game — from ToV _and_ Valda's Spire of Secrets (Renaissance era) — with some tweaks to the damage dice noted [[Guns|here]]; the bigger the boom, the more likely it is to be encountered in this world

Come in with the following assumptions:
- You each bear memberships to the [[Is'Deli Brotherhood]] pirate faction
- You set sail on [[Campaign NPCs#Captain Q#Ship]] under the command of [[Campaign NPCs#Captain Q]]
	- Each of you will have a role in the crew, granting standard abilities/equipment in the beginning of the campaign.
- You are all currently call the city of [[Grayport]] home
- 2 rumors and 1 truth have spread about you amongst your peers. The other player characters know of you and will have heard at least one of these


Ability Scores:
- Standard Array: 16, 14, 14, 13, 10, 8
OR
- Roll your stats: 4d6 drop the lowest. %% Any score <=16 can get a +2, any other score <= 17 can get a +1 %%

### Where do you come from?
There are eight cultural regions of the Cara-Duhnian isles. Choosing where you hail from determines which Linages/Heritages you have to choose from, what histories and religions you would be exposed to, and what Dialects (languages) you know. Each region also has its own special trait that you may want to consider:

- [[Erdú|Erdún clans]]
	The lands of Erdú are by far the least populated region in Duhn, filled with small bogs, sunlit glades, and lush forests. This nation is governed by a confederation of tribes who maintain the careful balance between nature and themselves, coming together when times of great need arise.
	- Choosing the Erdún clans as your native culture hub adds +1 Wis stat during character creation. 
	- Religions found here: • [[Doctrines#Kindleheart Concordia|Kindleheart Concordia]] • [[Vólat Church#Kilkesh Riots|Vólat-rah]] • [[Vólat Church#Kilkesh Riots|Vólat-sin]] • Iadizen • Astryan Pantheon
	- Lineages found [[Duhn#Inhabitants|here]]: [[Alseid]], [[Elvi]], [[Nagaji]], [[Hobgoblin]] (Wildings), [[Aqualii]] (Shae’thalis)
	- Common Heritages:

- [[House Tarahn|Tarahni Houses]] 
	- Choosing the region of the Houses of Tarahn as your native land allows you to roll any saving throw at advantage once per long rest.
	- The Tarahni are the second largest nation after the [[Carain Empire]]. It is an extremely feudal society ruled by four ruling Dukes, each responsible for governing their own land and laws that vary slightly from duchy to duchy. Their cooperation has often been their greatest strength, opening the nation to some of the most lucrative trade benefits in the known world. The wealth has recently led many to seek war, however, choosing conquest and expansionary goals over the simple increase of wealth accumulation. It has been a few years since their last great war, and it is only a matter of time before they set their sights on their northern neighbors. 
	- Religions found here: • [[Vólat Church#Kilkesh Riots|Vólat-rah]] • [[Doctrines#Thraev'gal Pact|Thraev'gal Pact]] • Zurlism • Segrilade 
	- Races found [[Duhn#Inhabitants|here]]: [[Syderean]], [[Smallfolk]] (Gnome), [[Elvi]] (High), [[Oread]] (Ifrit), [[Hobgoblin]]
	- Common Heritages:

- [[Vólatian Theocracy]]
	- Choosing the Theocracy as your native land grants you expertise in the Persuasion Skill and two extra languages of your choice. 
	- The Theocracy is, outwardly, the most peaceful nation and is often called “The land of influence” by those outside the realm. However, the [[Vólatian Theocracy#Benedictiary|Benedictiary]] is barely holding itself together with much infighting and political strife that has resulted in the creation of the [[Vólatian Theocracy#Inquisitorium|Inquisitorium]]. The recent Vólatian [[Vólat Church#Schism|schism]] has shaken this land and many suspect it will fall into deeper chaos soon. The isle of [[Lesser Isles#Subasa|Subasa]] lies within their borders and is home to the [[University Towards Enlightened Truth]], the premier college campus of the sacred isles, filled with the brightest scholars and their newest technologies. This institution boasts the greatest magical collegiate programs is the known world, where many a spellcaster and [[Class Primer#Mechanist|Mechanist]] has studied and thrived. Many licenses are procured here for practicing Arcane, and even some Wyrd, out in the world, respected by all governments within the sacred isles. 
	- Religions found here: • [[Doctrines#Occult|Occult]] • [[Vólat Church#Kilkesh Riots|Vólat-rah]] • Philosophy: Traphar 
	- Races found [[Cara#Inhabitants|here]]: All
	- Common Heritages:

- [[Carain Empire]]
	- Choosing the Carain Empire as your native land adds +1 to your Str. or Con. stat during character creation. 
	- The Empire is a harsh and unforgiving land, ruled by its Tsar monarch from the great Dwarven stronghold of [[Petyrgrad]]. They are the delvers of the richest mines in the isles, producing over 70% of the rare minerals and gems that fill the coffers of the Sunken Bank. 
	- Religions found here: • [[Doctrines#Occult|Occult]] • [[Vólat Church#Kilkesh Riots|Vólat-rah]] • Renaity • Segrilade 
	- Races found [[Cara#Inhabitants|here]]: [[Oread]], [[Hobgoblin]], [[Smallfolk]] (Gnome), [[Shix]], [[Urik]], [[Alseid]] (Tashi), [[Nagaji]] (Tashi)
	- Common Heritages:

- [[Ropeshian Dynasty]]
	- Choosing the Ropeshian Dynasty as your native land allows you to add your proficiency bonus to any rerolled saving throw once per long rest.
	- Religions found here: • [[Doctrines#Kindleheart Concordia|Kindleheart Concordia]] • [[Doctrines#Thraev'gal Pact|Thraev'gal Pact]] • [[Vólat Church#Kilkesh Riots|Vólat-rah]] • [[Vólat Church#Kilkesh Riots|Vólat-sin]] • Philosophy: Sha-Sha • Philosophy: Gulu

- [[Is'Deli Brotherhood]]
	- Choosing the dispersed pirate nation Is'Deli Brotherhood as your native culture grants you the ability to make an additional attack once per combat as a reaction. This combined coalition of the [[Delipol]] and [[Lesser Isles#Isthar|Isthar]] isles is filled with cutthroats, nerdowells, and pirates. Outsiders know little about this nation, though their threat to trade ships and wealthy nobles living easy on the coast is ever-present. Nonetheless, those that do come from this kingdom are always the wiliest, most crafty allies one can make.
