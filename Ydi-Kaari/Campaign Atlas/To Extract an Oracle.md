An oracle of [[Fate|Asteria]], the last of the living [[Valencia#The Plaehedies|Plaehedies]], resides in Delipol, keeping the shrine of [[Darkness|Vespera]] and watching over the holy sites of [[Nashtara]]. They are said to be capable of divining one truthful event from a person's life, past present or future, and sharing visions along with the cryptic messages weaved in Asteria's stars. Many Vólat-sin faithful used to make a pilgrimage to the oracle to seek favor or hear the stories of their gods from the lips of one that walked alongside them and spoke with the heroes of that age. Though now, in the days after the schism, the popularity of the oracle has sharply diminished.

Why the oracle might be found in [[Caergold]] during these most tense days? Perhaps they wanted to help quell the division brought on by the Vólat schism. To instill the light of hope in the hearts of the people once again and offer guidance to the heads of the [[House Tarahn|Tarahni Houses]].
