	- For those who want to flee the tides of war between the Empire and the [[House Tarahn|Tarahni Houses]], they must brave the influx of [[Leviathans]] that also are drawn to the spiritual power of the Isles.

A lone seaman—sailing under the command of a [[Campaign NPCs#Captain Q|legendary pirate captain]] on a deadly mission to break the back of the Imperial war machine—will be drawn overboard by the call of an otherworldly siren, clutching a strange-looking dagger inlaid with a small, glowing emerald. A great Spirit from the [[Planes of Existence|Harmonic Ethereal]] plane dies.
