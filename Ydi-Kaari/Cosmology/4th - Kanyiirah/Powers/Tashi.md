Led by their great Khans, 8 tribes of the nomadic Tashi people make up this roving power structure that freely wanders the riverbanks and steppes of the Carain heartland. These superstitious and unyielding clans of [Alseid](#customlist?id=4tgsen0le1mxxeko&type=Lineages), [Nagaji](#customlist?id=v1qf9st83th9in5e&type=Lineages), and [Hobgoblin](#customlist?id=ro2vdgd8qc46cscc&type=Lineages) outcasts that inhabit the unconquered lands between the Dynastic Ropeshian and Imperial Carain borders pay tribute to the Tashi Khans and their mighty Alseid warbands for protection from the troops that harry them. No matter their heritage, those who call the river plains their home are fiercely independent. They resist tooth, hoof, and nail against any effort to fence them in or drive them out.

Though they are not organized enough to be worth an all-out war from the Empire, they do raid enough of the border mines to stay on the Carain emperor's short list, while also extracting heavy tolls on the land trade routes and pitching camps on rich farm ground that the Ropeshians covet. These strained relatios keep the tensions amongst their neighbors at a constant high.

# Cultural Heritages
## Tashi Tribes
_**Stampeding Charge.**_ 
Once per long rest, when you hit with an attack after moving at least 15 feet on your turn, you can deal an additional **2d6** piercing damage from the momentum of the steppes you were raised in. 

_**Plains Survival.**_
You have proficiency with longbow, shortbow, and shortsword. In addition, you have proficiency in one of the following skills: Animal Handling, Survival, or Intimidation.

_**Languages.**_ 
You know the common Trade tongue and one additional language of your choice. Typical Tashi tribespeople choose Carain.

>Spirit Whispers. The superstitious traditions of the Tashi teach communion with ancestral spirits. You can cast the augury spell without requiring material components. Once you cast it, you must finish a long rest before you can cast it again. Your spellcasting ability for this spell is Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma (choose which during character creation). 

## Erdún Clans
A confederation of shamans and woodsmen who tend the ancient Gleamwood forest of western Duhn. These spiritual guardians live as monastic protectors of the forest, harvesting lumber with reverence and moderation under the watch and memory of Sylvania. It is well known throughout the isles that the quality of lumber exported from Erdún ports is unmatched anywhere else in the world.

The Erdún blend the power of primordial magic with attunement to the needs of the forest, ensuring the balance of the Gleamwood's sacred groves is protected from exploitation. They craft songs and weave oral traditions that harmonize with the forest's breath and maintain communion with the spirits that dwell within the primordial wood. Their communities rotate leadership among different family groups, with the great [Alseid](#customlist?id=4tgsen0le1mxxeko&type=Lineages) families holding chief positions alongside [Shix](#customlist?id=5rvhm1vz593oyjl4&type=Lineages) and [Nagaji](#customlist?id=v1qf9st83th9in5e&type=Lineages) councils.

_**Forest Craftwork**_. 
You have proficiency with one set of artisan's tools of your choice from the following: carpenter's tools, woodcarver's tools, or herbalism kit. When you craft a nonmagical wooden item during downtime, you decrease the number of workweeks it takes to complete the project by 1 week (minimum 1 day).

_**Gleamwood Communion.**_ 
Your attunement to the forest grants you affinity with its spirits. You know the druidcraft cantrip. In addition, you can cast the speak with animals spell without requiring material components. Once you cast speak with animals, you must finish a long rest before you can cast it again. Your spellcasting ability for these spells is Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma (choose which during character creation).

_**Languages**_. 
You know the common Trade tongue and one additional language of your choice. Typical Erdún clansfolk know Duhnian or Druidic
