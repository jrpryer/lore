Kanyiirah is the 4th of 6 observable spheres that orbit the star system's binary Suhns and is the vibrant, life-giving heart of the Ydi-Kaari system. Orbited itself by [[Moons|two moons]]

It is the world that holds the sacred isles of [[Cara-Duhn]], the [[Eternal Maelstrom]], and many other lands yet unknown... there are a total of 5 continents on the sphere, with much of the world left undiscovered by the inhabitants of the Cara-Duhnian islands.

# Calendar
The predominant calendar system of Cara-Duhn follows the dance of the twin moons, structured around Roida's consistent 28-day cycle while honoring the celestial events that mark the passing year.

**Year:** 380 days divided into 13 months 
**Standard Months:** Nine months of 28 days (252 days total) 
**Eclipse Months:** Three months of 32 days (96 days total) 
**Final Month:** One month of 32 days

## Months
**First Dancing** - 28 days - Valya bright, Roida awakening
**Dreamward** - 28 days - Both moons rising together
**Crimson Veil** - 32 days - _First Eclipse Month_ (The Eyes of Rhucryr in Majør)
**Pursuit** - 28 days - Valya appears to chase Roida
**Twin Zenith** - 28 days - Peak summer visibility
**Parting** - 28 days - Moons drift to opposite horizons
**Twin Crown** - 32 days - _Second Eclipse Month_ (Artyom's Scales in Majør)
**Red Dawns** - 28 days - Roida at Burning Zenith
**Violet Eves** - 28 days - Valya dominates while Roida dims
**Hearthward** - 28 days - Moons approach again
**Blood Convergence** - 32 days - _Third Eclipse Month_ (Whirlwind's Eye in Majør) - best viewing of [[Muéræ]]
**Shadow Dancing** - 28 days - Valya's shadowed phase
**Dwindlings** - 32 days - The Widow's Approach begins

## Eclipse Months
Each eclipse month contains the regular 28-day lunar cycle with an **additional four days** known as the binary Convergence days. During these periods, both Suhns align with the eclipse path, their combined gravitational influence creating observable effects:
- **Days 29-30: The Crimson Vigil** - Roida appears suspended in the sky, caught between the binary stars' pull. Tides run unusually high, and the crimson moon seems to pulse with intensified light.
- **Days 31-32: The Lusturil Window** - The gravitational distortion clears the night sky dramatically, allowing rare glimpses of Lusturil, the first sphere. Diviners and astrologers consider these the most auspicious nights for scrying and prophecy. Also offers some of the better views of [[Muéræ]]

### The Crimson Eclipse
Every 112 days (exactly 4 Roida cycles), Valya passes directly before her elder sibling, creating the **Crimson Eclipse**. This event falls precisely on the 14th night of the 3rd, 7th, and 11th months. The purple-white brilliance cutting across crimson light creates a striking halo effect considered sacred by most Cara-Duhnian cultures.

### The Widow's Approach
The 13th month coincides with the beginning of stellar shifts that, every seven years, culminate in the **Widow's Gaze**—when Roida and Valya align with Asteria's constellation. During Moon's Rest, Vespera's shadows recede slightly, creating the year's best conditions for astronomical observation and allowing navigators to chart courses by the distant light of neighboring star clusters.

### Cultural Observances
The calendar creates natural rhythms for Cara-Duhnian society:
- **Standard months:** Regular labor, trade, and travel
- **Eclipse months:** Festivals, religious observances, and rest from heavy labor
- **Binary Convergence Days:** Markets close, courts adjourn, and [[Elvi#Nox'eli|Nox'eli]] communities gather for twilight ceremonies
- **Moon's Rest:** Planning, divination, and preparation for the coming year

Each month contains exactly 8 complete dances of [[Moons#Valya|Valya]] (her 3.5-day cycle), creating a predictable pattern that even children can track: "Eight dances make a month, thirteen months complete the year, three eclipses mark the seasons."
