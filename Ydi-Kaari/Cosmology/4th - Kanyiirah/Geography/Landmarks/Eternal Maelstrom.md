# Mists
Great supernatural torrents of enchanted mists spew forth from the Eternal Maelstrom at all times, always reforming this sea-born barrier between the sacred isles of Cara-Duhn and the unexplorable lands beyond.

**References:**
Carain: the Zhek'ovl, "shield-wall" or "impenetrable"
Duhnian: the Hanlssperre, "barrier"
Deli: Shen Dao, "veiled path"
Qualissë: the Dolos, "colorless"

# History
Bound in a prison of shadow beneath the deadly vortex of the Maelstrom lies the force that wills the storm ever-churning: [[Borealis]], the [[Kanirûl Pantheon|Kanirûlian]] god with will of iron.
For it was both his own choice and the spirit of the Titan [[Umbravex]] itself that bound him there, even as the horrific body of the Titan was puppeted by the overwhelming telepathy of [[Entropy|Mykaedra's]] warmonger [[Catastrophe]]. 

During the climax of this battle between the monster and the gods, the spirit of Umbravex managed to slip its stasis and reached out to touch the mind of [[Lunatas]], warning her of Mykaedra's ultimate intent: to claim the power of the [[Kanirûl Pantheon|Kanirûl]] and consume the Soul of the sphere itself. It also informed her of Umbravex's own children that had been spawned in the months prior... those [[Leviathans]] whom Catastrophe now called to its aid and who would undoubtedly tip the scales in its favor and lower the likelihood of the god's victory. 

There was but a moment of deliberation in the minds of the gods before they rent the sky and split the sea: the moment of the Departure had arrived.

Borealis' sacrifice during the Departure was made to safeguard the fledgling nations that called the sacred isles home, using his own power to veil the horizon in a powerful enchanted mist and mixing with Umbravex's power to force the natural currents of the ocean to all flow around and away from the same boundary.
