The proverbial center of the cosmos, representing both the physical point of gravitational rotation in the perceivable galaxy, and metaphysical location where %%Chaos was born, %%the spheres and Suhns were formed, and time as we know it began.

Though its existence is still debated, many astronomical theories use it as the foundation to inform their models of the universe and its structure, as the most simple observations of the night sky reveal the wheeling of celestial bodies far beyond even the sight of the Walking Gods.
