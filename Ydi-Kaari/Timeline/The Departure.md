At a call from [[Tympestyr]] the [[Kanirûl Pantheon|Kanirûl]] joined forces just off the northern coast of [[Delipol]] on the 7th evening of the 4th month. There, with the light of the Suhns high in the sky, something occurred between the gathered gods and the Titan [[Umbravex]]: an battle that lasted until the dimming of the daylight into dusk, torrents of water and sea foam sprayed for hundreds of feet as the mighty did battle. At it's climax, a violent [[Eternal Maelstrom|maelstrom]] was inextricably formed at the site, pouring forth from its deadly center gouts of [[Eternal Maelstrom|enchanted mist]]. From that day, the walking gods of the [[Kanirûl Pantheon|Kanirûl]] were never seen nor heard from again, marking the second most formative day in the history of the sacred isles and becoming known simply as **The Departure**.
